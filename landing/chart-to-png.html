The URL

Point an <img> or Markdown image at a chart URL and the PNG renders automatically. 
<img src="https://chartbytes.meridian-digital.pro/chart?t=bar&d=12,19,8,24&labels=Q1,Q2,Q3,Q4&title=Sales" alt="Sales chart">

PNG vs SVG

PNG renders identically everywhere and needs no renderer — the safe default for email and PDF. SVG is crisp, small and scales, but some clients block it; use it for web pages and documentation.
